Why Your Landing Page Is Not Converting (And How to Fix It)

2026-05-19 7 min read AIHues Team

Common mistakes in indie dev landing pages, and the copy/framework changes that doubled our signups.

I have watched 20+ teams struggle with landing page conversion, and they all make the same three mistakes. The frustrating part? Each mistake takes less than five minutes to fix once you know what to look for.

Diagnosing the Real Problem

Most landing page conversion pain points fall into three categories: setup friction, decision fatigue, and feedback loops that are too long. The tricky part is that the symptoms look similar, but the fixes are completely different.

Setup friction feels like "this is too complicated." Decision fatigue feels like "I do not know which option to choose." Slow feedback loops feel like "I am not sure if this is working." Each requires a different intervention.

The Three Fixes That Actually Help

  • For setup friction: Use a managed service or starter template. Do not build from scratch unless you have a very specific reason.
  • For decision fatigue: Limit yourself to two options. Any more and analysis paralysis kicks in.
  • For slow feedback: Create a micro-test that runs in under 60 seconds. Long feedback loops kill momentum.

Your Next Step

Do not try to implement everything in this article at once. Pick one idea that resonated, apply it this week, and see what happens. The best landing page conversion advice is worthless until you test it in your own context.

Quick Win

Spend 15 minutes today auditing your current landing page conversion setup. Write down one thing to change this week. That small act will create more momentum than any amount of reading.
