The Essential Five

After extensive testing, here are the indie dev AI tools resources I actually use and recommend:

  1. The Foundation: Start with a solid understanding of the basics. Most advanced problems are actually basic problems in disguise.
  2. The Accelerator: One well-chosen tool can replace three mediocre ones. Invest time in finding the right fit, not the most popular one.
  3. The Safety Net: Build a simple rollback plan before you make any major changes. The 10 minutes you spend on this will save you hours later.
  4. The Feedback Loop: Set up a weekly review ritual. What worked? What did not? What should you try differently next week?
  5. The Network: Find two or three people who are slightly ahead of you in indie dev AI tools and check in with them monthly. Their hindsight is your foresight.

What I Would Do Differently

If I were starting over with indie dev AI tools today, I would spend less time reading and more time doing. The fastest way to learn is to build something small, break it, and fix it. Everything else is just preparation.

Takeaway

Start small. Measure obsessively. Optimize only what matters. Everything else is noise.
