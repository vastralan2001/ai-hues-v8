A Framework That Actually Works

After two years of trial and error, I have settled on a simple framework for read faster without losing comprehension. It is not revolutionary. It is not trendy. But it works for me, and it might work for you too.

Step 1: Start with the Outcome

Before doing anything, write down the one result you want. Not ten. One. "Increase signups by 20%" is specific. "Grow the business" is not. Specificity forces focus.

Step 2: Find the Bottleneck

Where is read faster without losing comprehension actually breaking down for you? Is it awareness? Conversion? Retention? Most people optimize the wrong stage. I spent months improving my landing page when my actual problem was that no one was visiting it.

Step 3: Make One Change

Change one thing. Run it for two weeks. Compare before and after. If it helps, keep it. If not, revert and try something else. The goal is progress, not perfection.

Common Mistakes I Made

Here is where I wasted the most time:

  1. Over-optimizing too early. I spent weeks perfecting a system that had no users yet. Premature optimization is not just a coding problem.
  2. Ignoring the data I had. I collected metrics but rarely looked at them. The answers were already there; I just was not paying attention.
  3. Chasing trends. Every new tool or technique seemed worth trying. Most of them added complexity without adding value.
