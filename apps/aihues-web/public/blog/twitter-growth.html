Twitter/X Growth Playbook: 0 to 10K in 90 Days

I grew from 200 to 10,000 followers in 90 days. No hacks. No bots. Just consistency and a few counter-intuitive strategies.

2026-05-188 min readAIHues Team

January 1st, 2026. I had 200 followers, most of them bots and my mom. I made a deal with myself: 90 days of showing up every single day. No excuses. No "I do not feel inspired today." Just raw consistency.

By April, I hit 10,000. Not viral. Not lucky. Just a system that worked. Here is exactly what I did — including the stuff that failed.

Phase 1: The Boring Foundation (Days 1-30)

Everyone wants to skip this part. Do not. The first 30 days are where most people quit, which is exactly why they matter.

I picked a niche so narrow it felt embarrassing: "SaaS pricing psychology for indie developers." Not "marketing." Not "SaaS." Just pricing psychology. Specific enough that I could own the conversation.

My daily rhythm was simple. Three posts. Every day. One thread (my best content), one quick tip, one reply to someone slightly bigger than me. That is it. No fancy tools. No scheduling software. Just me and the compose button.

The first two weeks? Crickets. I averaged 3 likes per post. My mom was still my top engager. But I kept going because I had made a deal with myself.

"Week 3 is where everyone quits. The initial excitement is gone, and the results have not shown up yet. Push through week 3."

Phase 2: The Reply Strategy (Days 31-60)

Here is the thing nobody tells you: your replies are more important than your original posts. Especially in the beginning.

I spent 45 minutes every morning replying to 20-30 tweets from accounts in the 1K-10K follower range. Not celebrities. Not accounts with 100K. Just people slightly ahead of me.

The key? Add value. Not "Great thread!" — that is worthless. I shared a relevant insight, asked a follow-up question, or told a 2-sentence story that added to their point.

About half of those people followed me back. Not because I asked. Because I actually contributed to their conversation.

My threads started getting traction too. Not viral. Just steady. 50 likes. Then 100. Then 200. The algorithm had noticed I was sticking around.

Phase 3: Monetization (Days 61-90)

By day 60, I had 4,000 followers. Not huge. But engaged. People were replying to my posts with their own stories. That is the sign you are building something real.

I launched a newsletter. Nothing fancy. ConvertKit free tier. I mentioned it at the end of my best-performing thread: "I write a weekly deep-dive on SaaS pricing. Link in bio if you are into that sort of thing."

200 people signed up in the first week. That blew my mind.

Then I created a Notion template — "SaaS Pricing Strategy Workbook" — and sold it for $19. I mentioned it once per week. No hard sell. Just: "Here is what I use. It helped me. Maybe it helps you."

First month revenue: $847. From a $19 Notion template. That is when I realized this could actually become a business.

What I Would Do Differently

I wasted too much time on my profile in the beginning. Spent 3 hours tweaking my bio. It did not matter. What mattered was posting.

I also tried engagement pods for one week. Worst week of the experiment. Fake engagement does not fool the algorithm. It just teaches it to ignore you.

And I wish I had started the newsletter earlier. Day 30 instead of day 60. Those 30 days of lost subscribers still haunt me.

The Real Secret

There is no secret. I know that is not what you want to hear. But it is the truth.

The people who hit 10K followers are not smarter than you. They are not more talented. They just did not stop. While everyone else quit at day 14, they kept posting. While everyone else got discouraged by low engagement, they kept replying.

Consistency compounds. Slowly at first. Then all at once.
