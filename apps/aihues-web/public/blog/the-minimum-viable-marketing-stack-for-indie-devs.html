The Minimum Viable Marketing Stack for Indie Devs

2026-05-05 7 min read AIHues Team

The 5 tools and 3 channels you need to get traction without a marketing team.

I have tried more minimal marketing stack approaches than I care to admit. Most were forgettable. A few were genuinely useful. And three completely changed how I work. Here is what made the cut — and why.

The Essential Five

After extensive testing, here are the minimal marketing stack resources I actually use and recommend:

  1. The Foundation: Start with a solid understanding of the basics. Most advanced problems are actually basic problems in disguise.
  2. The Accelerator: One well-chosen tool can replace three mediocre ones. Invest time in finding the right fit, not the most popular one.
  3. The Safety Net: Build a simple rollback plan before you make any major changes. The 10 minutes you spend on this will save you hours later.
  4. The Feedback Loop: Set up a weekly review ritual. What worked? What did not? What should you try differently next week?
  5. The Network: Find two or three people who are slightly ahead of you in minimal marketing stack and check in with them monthly. Their hindsight is your foresight.

Your Next Step

Do not try to implement everything in this article at once. Pick one idea that resonated, apply it this week, and see what happens. The best minimal marketing stack advice is worthless until you test it in your own context.

Quick Win

Spend 15 minutes today auditing your current minimal marketing stack setup. Write down one thing to change this week. That small act will create more momentum than any amount of reading.
